# ADR-0001: Initial v0.1 research boundary

- Status: PROPOSED
- Date: 2026-08-02
- Decision owners: project maintainers

## Context

QBitPlan investigates whether query-conditioned, interaction-aware bit
planning can preserve model quality under a resource budget. The initial
research brief motivates this direction but is unverified research input, not
an accepted specification or verified bibliography.

The research space includes many possible bit widths, granularities,
controllers, quantization targets, serving layouts, datasets, and hardware
backends. Attempting all of them in v0.1 would prevent clear falsification.
The scientific and reporting invariants for any later experiment live in
[SCIENTIFIC_STANDARDS.md](../../SCIENTIFIC_STANDARDS.md).

## Proposed decision

Define the first research contract as a deliberately small, reproducible
oracle-and-planning study. This proposal is not an implementation
authorization.

## Proposed v0.1 boundary

- weight-only quantization;
- bit widths `{4, 8}`;
- eight contiguous layer groups;
- one bit choice per group;
- single-query execution;
- independent MCKP as the mandatory additive baseline;
- one interaction-aware planning method;
- one query-only variant;
- one causally valid probe or feedback variant;
- an offline oracle;
- explicit separation of simulated and measured cost;
- no requirement for a custom serving engine or custom CUDA kernels.

The exact interaction-aware algorithm and controller architecture remain OPEN.

## Proposed Stage-1 evidence and claim boundary

Apply the evidence-class rule in
[SCIENTIFIC_STANDARDS.md](../../SCIENTIFIC_STANDARDS.md) at the level of each
claim and cost dimension. A composite result must not inherit a stronger class
from one of its inputs.

- **Analytical** evidence is permitted for profile enumeration, bit-budget or
  parameter-storage arithmetic, and other explicitly stated model calculations.
  It does not establish empirical quality, signal, interaction, oracle
  headroom, or measured systems benefit.
- **Simulated** evidence is permitted for signal, interaction, and oracle-
  headroom tests when the experiment uses the declared reference, real task
  targets, paired query sets, and a predeclared primary metric. It supports
  simulated quality/cost claims only; it does not establish measured memory,
  latency, or serving improvement.
- **Lookup-table estimated** evidence is permitted for cost estimates of
  profiles covered by a declared calibration table and manifest. It supports
  estimated cost comparisons only; it does not establish unobserved
  resident-memory, latency, prefetch-stall, kernel-switch, or controller-path
  behavior as measured.
- **Directly measured** evidence is required for a measured Stage-1 systems
  claim. The run must execute the declared profile on the UUID-pinned target
  device and backend, with the relevant instrumentation. Trace-derived stall,
  switch, and controller totals remain derived calculations from direct trace
  evidence and must identify their derivation and profiling overhead.

For signal, interaction, and oracle-headroom claims, simulated or directly
measured execution may support quality/degradation results; analytical evidence
is supporting arithmetic only, and lookup-table evidence is cost estimation only.
Therefore, signal, interaction, and oracle-headroom claims are bounded to
quality/degradation and explicitly labeled cost evidence; they do not imply a
systems benefit. Systems claims are made per dimension—resident accelerator
bytes, host-to-device bytes, latency, prefetch stalls, kernel switches, and
controller overhead—and only at the evidence strength available for that
dimension. A candidate-specific mixed-precision forward pass establishes path
execution only; it does not by itself establish task quality, memory/latency
benefit, or generalization.

Every reported dimension must include its evidence class, value or result,
calculation/measurement method, profile and run coverage, and a pointer to the
raw artifact. If a dimension is unavailable, unobserved, invalidated by
profiling or dropped records, or not replayed for an estimated profile, report
it as `omitted/unavailable` with the reason; do not report zero, impute it, or
upgrade another evidence class. A composite claim requiring an omitted
dimension is not established. Exact model, metric, threshold, and execution
semantics remain governed by the OPEN decisions below; the accepted cost
vector and comparison policy are recorded in
[ADR-0003](0003-stage-1-cost-contract.md).
## Deferred scope

Defer beyond v0.1:

- activation quantization;
- KV-cache quantization;
- 2-, 3-, and 6-bit execution;
- per-head and per-channel routing;
- dynamic batching;
- full token-level adaptation;
- linear-attention controllers;
- custom CUDA kernels;
- a large custom serving engine.

These are scope exclusions for the first contract, not claims that the
directions are unpromising.

## Rationale

The constrained formulation permits all 256 binary profiles to be enumerated
for a small eight-group pilot. This supports signal, interaction, and
oracle-headroom tests before investment in a more complex controller. It also
keeps the distinction between an executable bit profile and a measured
hardware benefit explicit.

## Consequences

- The initial result may not establish production serving viability.
- Any later implementation should optimize experimental clarity before serving
  throughput.
- Expanding the boundary requires a superseding ADR.
- A sophisticated controller should not be developed if the oracle offers
  little benefit over a static profile, unless a later ADR changes that
  premise.

## Research evidence record

On 2026-08-03, [Verify pilot model, revision, and quantization/backend
feasibility](https://github.com/weige15/QBitPlan/issues/3) recorded a
source-cited feasibility matrix in
[docs/research/issue-3-model-backend-feasibility.md](../research/issue-3-model-backend-feasibility.md).
The matrix retains four public 32-layer causal-model candidates for the later
model-selection decision and identifies TorchAO's documented per-FQN
weight-only APIs as the only reviewed path that directly covers mixed 4/8
group choices. Candidate-specific checks and the accepted model decision are
recorded in [ADR-0004](0004-stage-1-pilot-model.md). The exact execution
semantics, tokenizer/software tuple, and target-environment manifest remain
downstream decisions.

On 2026-08-03, [Verify Stage-1 hardware or proxy and observable cost
dimensions](https://github.com/weige15/QBitPlan/issues/5) recorded a
source-cited hardware/proxy matrix in
[docs/research/issue-5-hardware-cost-feasibility.md](../research/issue-5-hardware-cost-feasibility.md).
The evidence supports a conditional direct-measurement path using a pinned
NVIDIA CUDA device, the declared TorchAO/PyTorch path, CUDA activity tracing,
NVML/allocation measurements, and explicit NVTX ranges. A hardware-calibrated
lookup table remains a secondary estimated proxy; average-bit or
fake-quantized cost is not a systems result. This is feasibility evidence, not
a hardware, backend, or cost-contract selection. Exact device identity,
software tuple, prefetch schedule, and remaining protocol fields stay OPEN;
the accepted cost contract is recorded in
[ADR-0003](0003-stage-1-cost-contract.md).

On 2026-08-03, the maintainer selected direct measurement on the shared
NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 environment for the Stage-1 cost contract. Each run
uses one available RTX 3090, selected with `nvidia-smi` and pinned by the
reported GPU UUID; the eight-device inventory observed at selection time had
24,576 MiB per device, driver 580.159.03, CUDA 13.0, 0% utilization, 1--4 MiB
used, and no running processes. The execution path is CUDA with PyTorch and
TorchAO weight-only 4/8 support, subject to the later model and group-semantics
decisions. Direct cost evidence uses CUDA activity tracing with Nsight
Systems/CUPTI, NVML/allocation measurements, and explicit NVTX ranges. A
hardware-calibrated lookup table is not the Stage-1 contract; average-bit and
fake-quantized cost remain analytical or simulated evidence only. The exact
per-run UUID, package versions, profiling mode, prefetch schedule, and cost
definitions remain reproducibility or downstream protocol fields.

## OPEN decisions

The following choices are intentionally unresolved and must not be inferred
from this ADR:

- final execution and reproducibility manifest details remain OPEN; the
  accepted model, reference, task, metric, and cost decisions are consolidated
  in [ADR-0015](0015-stage-1-consolidated-protocol.md);
- quantization implementation or library;
- Python environment, dependencies, and orchestration stack;
- exact per-run measurement scopes and later per-dimension budget ceilings
  under [ADR-0003](0003-stage-1-cost-contract.md);
- interaction-aware algorithm and controller architecture;
- number of profile medoids or library size;
- serving layout and any prefetch/cache policy.

The Stage-1 primary and diagnostic metric policy is accepted in
[ADR-0008](0008-stage-1-primary-and-diagnostic-metrics.md). Later experiments
must still predeclare the implementation-level measurement details listed in
[ADR-0015](0015-stage-1-consolidated-protocol.md).

## Acceptance condition

Change this ADR from PROPOSED to ACCEPTED only after the maintainers confirm
that the v0.1 boundary and its deferrals are intentional. Acceptance of this
ADR does not accept any item listed under OPEN.
